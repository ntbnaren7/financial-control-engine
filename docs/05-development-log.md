# Development Log

This document records the **actual state of the implementation**.

It is not a design document.

The repository, tests, and observed provider behavior are authoritative over this log.

---

## Entry Format

### YYYY-MM-DD — [Short Title]

#### Completed

- What was actually implemented.

#### Verified

- What was actually tested.
- Include commands, scenarios, or provider observations where useful.

#### Changed

- Important architectural or behavioral changes.

#### Known Issues

- Known bugs
- incomplete behavior
- temporary shortcuts
- unresolved questions

#### Decisions

- Decisions made based on implementation evidence.

#### Next

- The immediate next engineering objective.

---

## Rules

1. Record facts, not intentions.
2. Do not claim functionality that has not been tested.
3. Record provider behavior discovered during implementation.
4. Record important failures and their fixes.
5. Record temporary shortcuts that must later be replaced.
6. Keep entries concise.
7. Never use this document as a substitute for tests.
8. Update the log after meaningful vertical-slice milestones.
